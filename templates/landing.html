Scoped to your account

Every read and write is keyed to your account. Another user's conversation id returns a 404, not a transcript.

Hardened sessions

Signing in discards all prior session state, so a planted cookie cannot survive into your session. Every state change requires a CSRF token.

No third parties

A strict content security policy locks scripts, styles and frames to this origin. Fonts are self-hosted. There are no trackers.
